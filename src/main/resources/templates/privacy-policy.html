Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

UPSC Tracker is designed to help users organize, track and improve their UPSC preparation. This policy explains what information the application collects, why it is used, how it is intended to be protected, and what choices users have regarding their information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, UPSC Tracker may store the following information:

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • UPSC preparation information such as attempt year, motivation, dream post, education, profession, location and profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Study-tracking information such as subjects, topics, completion status, revision information and preparation progress.
  • Feedback that you voluntarily submit through the application.
  • AI chatbot conversations that you choose to create.
  • Files or images that you voluntarily upload to the AI chatbot for processing.

We do not need information that is unrelated to the application's educational and account-management functions. Please avoid uploading highly sensitive personal information to the chatbot.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your UPSC Tracker account.
  • Save your preparation progress and preferences.
  • Provide revision, performance and study-tracking features.
  • Provide AI chatbot responses based on your request and the information you choose to provide.
  • Process feedback and improve the application.
  • Help protect the application from misuse, unauthorized access and security incidents.

3. AI Chatbot and Uploaded Content

The AI chatbot may process messages, selected profile information and files that you intentionally provide in order to generate a response.

Do not upload passwords, financial information, identity documents, authentication codes or other highly sensitive information to the chatbot.

Uploaded content should be treated as information provided for the requested chatbot functionality and should not be treated as a secure long-term document-storage service.

4. Data Sharing

UPSC Tracker does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate application features, such as hosting, database services, file storage, email delivery or AI processing.

Such processing should be limited to information reasonably necessary for providing the relevant service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of users or the application.

5. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information.

  • Passwords should be stored using secure one-way hashing, rather than as readable text.
  • User-specific data should be restricted to authenticated users and authorized application functionality.
  • Authentication-protected routes should be used to reduce unauthorized access to account information.
  • Uploaded files should be validated and restricted to supported file types and sizes where applicable.
  • API endpoints should validate input and reject unauthorized requests where applicable.

No internet-based application can guarantee absolute security. We aim to continuously reduce security risks and respond appropriately to identified vulnerabilities.

6. Data Retention

Account and preparation data may be retained while your account remains active or while it is reasonably needed to provide application functionality.

When a deletion feature is provided, users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data, subject to legitimate legal or operational requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Access View the personal information associated with your account.
Correction Update information that is inaccurate or outdated.
Deletion Request deletion of account-related personal information.
Choice Avoid providing optional profile information or optional chatbot attachments.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

UPSC Tracker may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage where necessary for login sessions, preferences, security or application functionality.

We do not use unnecessary tracking technologies merely to monitor users' activity outside the application.

9. Children and Minors

The application is intended for users who are able to independently manage an online account. Users who are minors should use the service with appropriate parental or guardian involvement where required by law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application functionality, legal requirements or security practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, requests or concerns, users should contact the UPSC Tracker administrator through the contact method provided by the application.

Please include enough information to identify your account or request, but never send your password or authentication codes.
